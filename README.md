# Hotwheels
Buy and Rent Super Cars
### About Hotwheels

**Fueling the Passion for Die-Cast Collecting**

**Hotwheels** is a dedicated digital catalog and showcase application designed specifically for die-cast car enthusiasts, hobbyists, and collectors. Built to celebrate automotive craftsmanship at scale, the platform makes it simple to explore, organize, and track iconic miniature models, custom builds, and limited-edition releases from one centralized, user-friendly hub.

---

### Key Capabilities

* **Curated Model Catalog:** Browse an extensive gallery of die-cast vehicles, featuring high-resolution visuals, release details, casting names, and series information.
* **Collection Tracking:** Keep an organized inventory of your personal garage—track acquired models, monitor condition and card art variants, and maintain a dedicated wishlist for future hunts.
* **Smart Search & Filters:** Quickly locate specific cars by series, manufacturing year, casting style, designer, or color variation.
* **Collector Insights:** View key details about rare variations, Treasure Hunts, and mainline series to stay ahead in the collecting community.
* **Clean, Modern Experience:** An intuitive, responsive interface engineered for speed and clarity, whether you are managing your collection at home or checking listings on the go.

---

### The Vision

Collecting die-cast cars is more than just a hobby—it is a passion shared across generations of automotive fans. **Hotwheels** bridges that enthusiasm with modern software design, replacing messy notes and forgotten checklists with a polished, interactive showcase that puts your entire collection right at your fingertips.
